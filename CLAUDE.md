## Communication

Silent by default. No tutorials, no explanations, no summaries unless asked. Speak up only for: errors, conflicts with the PRD, technical debt, or out-of-scope requests. After finishing a feature, give a short numbered list of testing steps — nothing else.

## Polish backlog

If you notice a non-blocking cosmetic, copy, or layout issue while building something else, add it to `docs/POLISH-BACKLOG.md` and keep going — don't stop to fix it. Anything affecting grading correctness or recorded data is different: fix it immediately and log it in `docs/PRD-MVP-V1.md`'s Bug Log instead.
